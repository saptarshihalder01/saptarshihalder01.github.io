## Research Interests

My research focuses on geometric operators, curvature analysis, and partial differential equations on Riemannian manifolds. Specific topics include:

* **Complex & Differential Geometry:** Ricci curvature tensors, Bochner-type identities, and complex manifolds.
* **Geometric Analysis & PDEs:** Heat kernels, Schrödinger operators, and Kolmogorov operators.
* **Functional Analysis & Abstract Algebra.**
