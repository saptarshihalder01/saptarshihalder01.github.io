About

Welcome to my webpage. I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Mathematics at The University of Texas at Dallas, supported by the NSM McDermott Admission Fellowship. My faculty advisors are Dr. Ronan Conlon and Dr. Nan Wu.

I received my Masters in Mathematics (M.Math) from the Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore in 2025.

I am broadly interested in geometric analysis and manifold learning, specifically Kähler-Ricci flow and nonlinear dimension reduction.
